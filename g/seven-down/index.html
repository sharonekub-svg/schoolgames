Seven Down

Puzzles

About Seven Down

Seven Down is a rolling-die puzzle game with a rule you can hold in one sentence: the face touching the ground is your number, and opposite faces always add up to seven — so the number you cannot see is always the one you can work out.

How to play

The Number You Can't See Roll a die across a board — and the number that counts is the face touching the ground, the one you can never see. Opposite sides of a die always add up to seven, so the hidden number is always the one you can work out. Three games are built on that single rule: 60 puzzle boards, 120 chased action stages, and 60 mazes drawn only where you have already been. Desktop Controls: ↑ ↓ ← → (Arrow keys) or W A S D: Roll the die. In the action stages the die keeps rolling for as long as you hold the key. 1: Bridge — lay a crossing over a gap. 2: Guard — blunt the spikes. 3: Shield — survive one hit. C: Change camera. V: Overview — pull back and see the whole board. Spacebar: Skip the countdown. Esc: Menu. Mobile Controls: Drag anywhere: Roll the die in that direction. Tap a power chip: Use bridge, guard or shield. Everything else is on screen — there is nothing to memorise.
