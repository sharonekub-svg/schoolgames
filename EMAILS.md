# Licensing emails — ready to send

The games you keep asking for are not in any catalogue we can reach. They are not
missing by accident; they are licensed exclusively elsewhere. The only legitimate
way to get them is to ask the people who own them.

That is exactly how Friv built its catalogue: it makes almost no games itself and
buys non-exclusive licences from independent developers.

**You send these, not me.** Fill in the bracketed parts. Run any actual agreement
or payment past a parent before you sign or pay anything.

---

## Before you send anything

Have these three things ready, or the emails are a waste of everyone's time:

1. **A live site at a real domain.** Nobody licenses a game to a localhost folder.
2. **A real traffic number.** Search Console, 30 days. If it is 400 sessions, say
   400. Do not invent a number — they can check, and a small honest figure reads
   far better than one that collapses under a single question.
3. **A parent who knows this is happening**, because a licence is a contract and
   you cannot sign one.

Send them one at a time, in the order below. Ranked by how likely you are to get
a yes.

>

> Thanks,

## What to expect

Most will not reply. That is normal and not personal — Friv reviews thousands of
games a year to pick a handful.

If someone says no, thank them and stop. Chasing gets you blacklisted with the
exact people you need later.

And do not take the "Fireboy And Watergirl Online" listing on GameMonetize as a
substitute. That title is exclusively licensed, so an entry using that name on an
open broker feed is a clone wearing someone else's trademark. The takedown lands
on your domain, not the broker's.
