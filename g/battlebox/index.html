About BattleBox

Battle Box is ad Idle game where the more you risk, the more you get! Try to collect all the jewels in each level and to memorize their location! Be careful tough! Under some blocks there is a devil that will hurt you! If you find three of them the game will be over! By the way, you can restore your health if you find a strawberry, so keep an eye on it.
