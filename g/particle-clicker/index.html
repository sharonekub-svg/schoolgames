About Particle Clicker

An idle game where you run a particle physics lab. Click to gather data, hire researchers, upgrade the detector, and unlock discoveries. Built by physicists at CERN's summer school, so the science underneath it is real.

How to play

Click the detector to collect data. Spend it on staff and upgrades.
