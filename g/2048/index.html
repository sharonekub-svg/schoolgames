About 2048

Slide the numbered tiles with the arrow keys. Two tiles of the same number merge into their sum. Keep merging until you reach 2048 - then keep going and see how far past it you can get.

How to play

Arrow keys to slide all tiles in one direction. On a phone, swipe.
