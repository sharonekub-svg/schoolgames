How to play

The goal of the game is to destroy all zombies and monsters. Find new weapons in the boxes. Upgrade characters and weapons to defeat strong enemies. Q (K) - change weapons; W, A, S, D (arrows) - move; E (M) - apply / activate; F (J) - super weapon. X, Space (L, KeyPad 0) - shooting; Mouse - look / shoot; For mobile devices, use touch input.
