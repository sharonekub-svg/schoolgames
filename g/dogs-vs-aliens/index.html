About Dogs vs Aliens

Your favorite pets have been kidnapped by evil aliens! Hurry to save them and punish the alien bullies. Evolve to defeat stronger enemies, free new dogs, get unique resources so that your dogs can evolve! Collect all the dog breeds and see how cool they will be! Discover new territories, visit the beach, park, city square and forest!

How to play

To win, you need to save all the pets and send the aliens back to the Moon! Get resources and upgrade your dogs. Hand over enemies to the base Controls: PC: WASD - move (or use the mouse), Attack - automatic when approaching the enemy. Mobile - use your finger to move, Attack - automatic when approaching the enemy.
