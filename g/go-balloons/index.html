About Go Balloons!

A fairground squirrel sells balloons — until a gust steals them skyward. There’s only one thing left to do: climb after them.

How to play

1. Press Start at the booth — balloons soar, the squirrel clings to a wall. 2. Tap (or press Space) to leap to the opposite wall. 3. Catch balloons in flight; gold ones are worth more. 4. Dodge ledges and pigeons. 5. After a fall, check height and balloons, beat today's record, and try again. Goal: maximize height per run. Height feeds the leaderboard; balloons fuel achievements.
