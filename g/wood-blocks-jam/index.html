About Wood Blocks Jam

Wood Blocks Jam is a relaxing logic puzzle game with colorful wooden blocks. Your goal is to drag each block into a gate of the same color. No time limits — just calm, thoughtful gameplay.

How to play

Wood Blocks Jam is easy to learn and fun to master: 📱 On mobile: Drag blocks with your finger and drop them into the matching color gates. 🖱️ On desktop: Click and hold the left mouse button to grab a block, then drag it to the correct gate. Your goal is to clear the board by matching each colored block with its corresponding gate. As you progress, the puzzles get trickier — introducing new mechanics, obstacles, and challenges. Think ahead, plan your moves carefully, and don’t forget to use boosters if you’re stuck!
