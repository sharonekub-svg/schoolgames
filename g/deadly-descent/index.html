About Deadly Descent

Drive your car down the mountain and get to the end of the track in one piece!

How to play

"Deadly Descent" is a simulator of driving down a mountain by car. You need to get to the finish line.. The difficulty is that there are a large number of obstacles on the track, a collision with which will destroy your car, so you should drive very carefully. This driving simulator is for those who enjoy different types of car destruction. Now you can complete them on your phone or computer completely free of charge. The game features destructible cars and realistic driving simulation. Despite the well-developed physics and realistic graphics, the game does not require many resources from the device and will give you a realistic driving experience on any device. Compete with other players! Each unique track has a table of records, where each player automatically records his best result - the maximum distance that your car was able to travel on the track.
