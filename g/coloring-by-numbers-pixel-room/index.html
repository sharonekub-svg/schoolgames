About Coloring by Numbers. Pixel Room

Immerse yourself in a game where the process of coloring becomes truly meditative. Create and decorate houses as a designer. Just touch the screen and the colors will match the numbers in the image. Forget about boring routine - now you are thinking about color combinations. For even more relaxation, a music player has been added.

How to play

The goal of the game is to design unique rooms using meditative coloring by numbers. Focus on color schemes by tapping on the picture. Control - touch / left mouse button.
