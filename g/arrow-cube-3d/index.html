About Arrow Cube 3D

Remove every arrow from the six-sided cube by finding the perfect order. Arrow Cube Escape is a calm 3D logic puzzle that challenges your focus, planning, and spatial thinking in short, satisfying levels.

How to play

Tap an arrow to remove it from the cube. An arrow can move only when nothing blocks its path. Rotate and inspect the cube, choose the correct order, and clear all arrows to complete the level.
