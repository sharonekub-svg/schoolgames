About SkiFree

The endless downhill skier from the old Windows entertainment pack, rebuilt in JavaScript. Dodge trees and rocks, go as far as you can, and try to outrun the yeti that eventually comes for you.

How to play

Arrow keys or click where you want to go. F to speed up.
