About Picture Shift

One board. A pile of mismatched blocks. A picture hiding inside them.

How to play

🎯 Goal — Rebuild every picture before the timer hits zero. Targets sit at the top; their pieces are scattered across several blocks. Line them up correctly relative to each other and the picture snaps together on its own. They don't have to be in any particular spot on the board — just in the right formation. 🖱️ Controls — Drag a block. That's it. Blocks slide through empty space only. For boosters: tap the booster → tap your target (valid blocks glow). 💀 You lose if the timer runs out or a bomb hits zero. Costs a life — but you can revive on the spot. 🧠 Tips — Think backwards (which block moves last?). Shrink, don't shove (Hammer beats rearranging). Kill bombs early. Chase pictures, not tidiness — every completed picture clears blocks and thaws ice.
