About Asteroids

The 1979 vector arcade cabinet, rebuilt in HTML5 canvas. Rotate, thrust, and shoot the rocks apart - each hit splits them into faster, smaller pieces. Pure momentum and no brakes.

How to play

Arrow keys to rotate and thrust. Space to fire.
