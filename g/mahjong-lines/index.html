About Mahjong Lines

Mahjong Lines is an engaging logic puzzle game in the mahjong connect genre, where you need to find and connect pairs of matching tiles using simple and intuitive rules.

How to play

The rules of Mahjong Lines are simple and intuitive: 🀄Tap on two identical tiles. 🀄Tiles can be removed if a path of no more than three straight lines can be built between them. 🀄The connecting line may pass only through empty cells. 🀄The connected pair disappears from the board. 🀄To complete a level, you need to remove all tiles. 🀄Use hints if you can’t find available moves and clear the board step by step, carefully planning each connection.
