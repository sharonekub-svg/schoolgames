Chicken Merge 2

About Chicken Merge 2

Chicken Merge 2 is a merge-meets-tower-defense game where your feathered army stands between your base and endless waves of enemies! Train, upgrade, and merge your chickens to create powerful defenders, then position them strategically on the battlefield. The stronger your flock, the better weapons and upgrades you’ll unlock! Can your ultimate chicken squad hold the line?

How to play

Train units and merge them to get stronger units, start the mission and clear the wave to get extra coins, these coins will help you train more units. Make you place most elite units on defense positions as they the ones that will attack at the enemies. Use power ups wisely and timely as they have a cool down. Desktop Controls: Use mouse click to interact with game ui to train units. Drag and drop units of same level to merge them Drag and drop units to move them on different slots Mobile Controls: Use screen touch to interact with game ui to train units. Drag and drop units of same level to merge them Drag and drop units to move them on different slots
