About Pocket Tile

Pocket Tile is a compact number-merging puzzle that is easy to learn and rewarding to master. Place tiles on a 5×5 board, connect exactly three adjacent tiles with the same number and color, and merge them into a higher-value tile. Choose where the merged tile remains and plan ahead to keep space available.

How to play

1. Place the current tile in any empty space on the 5×5 board. The Next tile preview shows what is coming. 2. Connect exactly three orthogonally adjacent tiles with the same number and color. Diagonal tiles do not connect. 3. Select the three matching tiles, then tap one of them again to choose where the upgraded tile will remain. 4. Three 1 tiles merge into a 2, three 2 tiles merge into a 3, and so on. A normal merge scores the value of the new tile. 5. Mint tiles are marked with a circle and violet tiles with a diamond. Both the number and color must match. 6. Break tiles point toward a neighboring tile. They lower a matching-color number by one, or remove it if its value is 1. If no valid target is present, the Break tile waits until one arrives. 7. Clone tiles copy the matching-color number in the space they point toward. They also wait if no valid number is present. 8. Keep placing and merging until there are no legal placements or merges left. In Adventure, complete the displayed tile goal before the board runs out of moves. Controls: tap or click tiles and buttons. No keyboard controls are required.
