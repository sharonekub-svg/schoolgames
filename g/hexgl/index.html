About HexGL

A futuristic anti-gravity racer built in WebGL. You pilot a ship down a neon canyon track at absurd speed, scraping walls and hunting a clean lap. One of the best-looking games that runs in a browser tab.

How to play

Arrow keys or WASD to steer. Shift to boost, space to brake.
